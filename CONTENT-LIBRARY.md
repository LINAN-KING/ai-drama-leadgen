# Content Library

Content variants are deterministic data, not free-form runtime invention. Hooks, narration patterns, CTA phrasing, workbench labels, and shot intents carry stable IDs and truth constraints.

The script layer may rephrase only claims grounded in user input or observable workflow facts. It cannot introduce revenue, client, price, gift, feature, or performance claims. CTA copy preserves the user's action and offer.

Batch variants rotate hooks, narration, CTA expression, shot order, and licensed assets under a seed. Recent asset-use counts lower selection scores across batches. Consecutive shots cannot have highly similar subject and composition signatures.
