# Design

## Style Prompt

A cinematic production console where warm editorial storytelling meets exact machine state: dense, credible controls; strong hierarchy; restrained motion; and real inputs, processing, output previews, focus changes, and completion states.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#11100F`
- Raised surface: `#201D1A`
- Primary text: `#F5F0E8`
- Secondary text: `#BDB4A8`
- Action and completion accent: `#F05A47`

## Typography

- Display and Chinese UI: `Microsoft YaHei`, 800
- Data and controls: `IBM Plex Mono`, 350/600

## What NOT to Do

- No blue-purple gradients, neon dashboard styling, decorative cards, or rounded pills.
- No static screenshot masquerading as a process.
- No infinite animation, random time-based values, tiny web typography, or forced line breaks.
- No layout that depends on a single aspect ratio.
